This is the official WooCommerce extension to receive payments using the Gabon Sing Pay payments provider.

== Description ==

The SingPay extension for WooCommerce enables you to accept payments including Subscriptions, Deposits & Pre-Orders via one of Gabon’s most popular payment gateways.

= Why choose SingPay? =

SingPay gives your customers more flexibility including putting down deposits, ordering ahead of time or paying on a weekly, monthly or annual basis.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this require a SingPay merchant account? =

Yes! A SingPay merchant account, merchant key and merchant ID are required for this gateway to function.

= Does this require an SSL certificate? =

An SSL certificate is recommended for additional safety and security for your customers.

= Where can I find documentation? =

For help setting up and configuring, please refer to our [user guide](https://client.singpay.ga/doc/reference/index.html#/Paiement/post_ext)
